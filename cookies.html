Last Revised: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Codehelper Ai Solutions Private Limited (CIN: U62013RJ2025PTC103506) uses cookies and similar storage technologies on codehelper.us.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact text files stored on your device by your web browser when visiting web domains. They allow websites to remember session state, authenticate authorized users, and maintain preferences.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for standard website navigation, form submission security (CSRF protection), and server load balancing. These cookies do not require prior consent.
  • Functional Session Tokens: Used during auditor onboarding and verification workflows to ensure uninterrupted multi-step verification sessions.
  • Performance & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic telemetry to maintain high system availability and track technical errors.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Certain interactive components—such as the secure identity verification interface—may initialize necessary functional cookies to confirm session validity. We ensure our technical partners adhere to industry data protection frameworks.

4. Managing Browser Preferences

You can adjust your cookie settings through your browser preferences. Please note that restricting strictly necessary cookies may impact the proper functioning of the verification portal.

Questions regarding our use of cookies can be directed to support@codehelper.us.
