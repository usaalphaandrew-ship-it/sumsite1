1. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide 1-on-1 tutoring sessions, process payments, and ensure community safety:

  • Student Information: Name, professional/student email, learning goals, technical focus areas, and code repositories voluntarily submitted for pair-programming reviews.
  • Mentor Candidate Information: Full legal name, professional email, engineering experience, GitHub/portfolio links, and government identity documents processed securely during tutor verification.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, session cookies, and diagnostic logs to ensure high availability and security.

2. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal and technical data for legitimate operational and educational objectives:

  • To match students with verified senior mentors specializing in their requested programming languages and architecture tracks;
  • To verify the identity and credentials of prospective tutors before approving them to accept 1-on-1 sessions;
  • To facilitate secure payment transactions and issue official invoices;
  • To diagnose platform performance, prevent fraudulent activities, and comply with legal requirements.

3. Mentor Identity Verification & Compliance

To uphold student safety and instructional quality, all technical mentors complete an identity verification check. This check is processed securely by an authorized identity verification provider acting under contractual data protection terms. Verification documents are evaluated solely to establish identity and are secured with enterprise-grade encryption.

4. Student Code & Project Privacy

Your source code, prompts, and project designs remain your private property:

  • We do not share, sell, publish, or expose student repository contents to unauthorized third parties.
  • Mentors review code solely during active tutoring sessions and for preparing instructional feedback.
  • Student code is never used to train automated public LLMs or machine learning models.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose data only to trusted infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, payment processors, and identity verification services) strictly as needed to operate the service, or when mandated by applicable law.

6. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict access control policies, encrypted database storage, and continuous dependency vulnerability scanning.

7. Your Data Subject Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records. To exercise any data rights, please email our privacy team at support@aitutor.host.
